## Research Schema Add-on

- Apply the research source profile only to compatible literature, reports,
  theses, and datasets.
- Preserve study design, sample or corpus, measures, methods, and limitations.
- Omit unavailable or inapplicable optional bibliography; never invent it.
- Keep author claims, evidence strength, and agent interpretation distinct.
- Add locators to important quantitative, high-impact, or contested claims.
- Preserve unresolved disagreement with `status: contested`.
